How each state handles tips

  • California: California does not allow a tip credit. Tipped workers must be paid the full state minimum wage, and tips go on top.
  • Washington: Washington does not allow a tip credit. Tipped workers must be paid the full state minimum wage, and tips go on top.
  • New York: New York sets tipped cash wages by industry and region in its wage orders. Because the base rate isn't rising on January 1, 2027, the tipped rates tied to it stay where they are.
  • New Jersey: New Jersey's tipped cash wage rises with the minimum wage; the maximum tip credit stays at $9.87.
  • Connecticut: Connecticut sets fixed cash wages in law: $6.38/hr for hotel and restaurant servers and $8.23/hr for bartenders. They don't rise with the minimum wage, so the tip credit grows instead.
  • Michigan: Under Michigan's 2025 law, the tipped cash wage is 42% of the minimum wage in 2027, rising 2 points a year until it reaches 50% in 2031.
  • Rhode Island: Rhode Island's tipped cash wage is fixed at $3.89/hr in law. It does not rise with the minimum wage, so the tip credit grows to $13.11.
  • Colorado: Colorado lets employers count up to $3.02/hr of tips toward the minimum wage.
  • Virginia: Virginia uses the federal $2.13/hr tipped cash wage, which doesn't rise with the state minimum wage. The tip credit grows to $11.62.
  • Arizona: Arizona lets employers count up to $3.00/hr of tips, so the tipped cash wage will be $3.00 below whatever the 2027 rate turns out to be.
  • Ohio: Ohio's tipped cash wage is half of the state minimum wage for employers grossing $419,000 or more.
  • Maine: Maine's tipped cash wage is 50% of the state minimum wage. If tips don't make up the rest, the employer must.
  • Vermont: Vermont's tipped cash wage is half of the state minimum wage, so it will be set when the 2027 rate is announced.
  • Nebraska: Nebraska's tipped cash wage is fixed at $2.13/hr and did not change with the 2022 ballot measure or LB 258.
  • Minnesota: Minnesota does not allow a tip credit. Tipped workers must be paid the full state minimum wage, and tips go on top.
  • Montana: Montana does not allow a tip credit for businesses that pay the standard rate. Tipped workers get the full state minimum wage, and tips go on top.
  • South Dakota: South Dakota's tipped cash wage is half of the state minimum wage.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tip credit?

A tip credit is the part of the minimum wage an employer is allowed to cover with the worker's tips. The employer pays the lower cash wage, and if tips don't bring the worker up to the full minimum wage for the pay period, the employer has to pay the difference.

Which states don't allow a tip credit?

Of the states on this page, California, Washington, Minnesota, Montana require the full minimum wage before tips. Alaska, Nevada, and Oregon also ban tip credits but change their rates on other dates.

What is the federal tipped minimum wage in 2027?

$2.13 an hour, unchanged since 1991. Employers can use it only where state law doesn't set a higher cash wage.
