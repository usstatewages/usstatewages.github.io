Frequently asked questions

Why isn't New York's exact 2027 rate available yet?

Switches from a legislated schedule to annual CPI-W (Northeast) indexing starting January 2027. States using this kind of formula typically publish the next-year figure between September and December. New York hasn't published its 2027 figure yet, so this page shows the mechanism instead of a guessed number.

Does this apply to tipped workers?

Not necessarily. Many states, including New York, let employers pay tipped workers a lower direct cash wage as long as tips bring total pay up to at least the full minimum wage. The rate on this page is the standard rate for non-tipped workers - check your state labor department for the tipped cash wage.

Is this different from the federal minimum wage?

Yes. The federal minimum wage has been $7.25/hour since 2009. New York's minimum wage is higher, and employers covered by both laws must pay the higher of the two.

When does the new rate take effect?

January 1, 2027, alongside minimum wage increases in the other states tracked on this site.
