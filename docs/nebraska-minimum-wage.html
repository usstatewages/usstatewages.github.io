How the 2027 rate is set

Fixed 1.75% increase every January 1 starting in 2027, under LB 258 (in effect since July 17, 2026), which replaced the 2022 ballot measure's inflation formula ($15.00 x 1.0175 = $15.2625, rounded to $15.26).

A full-time worker (2,080 hours/year) at the new Nebraska minimum wage would earn about $31,741/year gross, up from about $31,200/year in 2026.

Source: Nebraska Department of Labor

Frequently asked questions

How was Nebraska's 2027 rate calculated?

Fixed 1.75% increase every January 1 starting in 2027, under LB 258 (in effect since July 17, 2026), which replaced the 2022 ballot measure's inflation formula ($15.00 x 1.0175 = $15.2625, rounded to $15.26). Nebraska's minimum wage is rising from $15.00 to $15.26 per hour.

Does this apply to tipped workers?

Not necessarily. Many states, including Nebraska, let employers pay tipped workers a lower direct cash wage as long as tips bring total pay up to at least the full minimum wage. The rate on this page is the standard rate for non-tipped workers - check your state labor department for the tipped cash wage.

Is this different from the federal minimum wage?

Yes. The federal minimum wage has been $7.25/hour since 2009. Nebraska's minimum wage is higher, and employers covered by both laws must pay the higher of the two.

When does the new rate take effect?

January 1, 2027, alongside minimum wage increases in the other states tracked on this site.
