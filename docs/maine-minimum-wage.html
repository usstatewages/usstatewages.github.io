How the 2027 rate is set

Annual cost-of-living adjustment based on the change in the CPI-W from August to August.

A full-time worker (2,080 hours/year) at the new Maine minimum wage would earn about $32,656/year gross, up from about $31,408/year in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How was Maine's 2027 rate calculated?

Annual cost-of-living adjustment based on the change in the CPI-W from August to August. Maine's minimum wage is rising from $15.10 to $15.70 per hour.

What is Maine's tipped minimum wage in 2027?

Maine's tipped cash wage is 50% of the state minimum wage. If tips don't make up the rest, the employer must. The lowest cash wage before tips in 2027 is $7.85/hr.

Is this different from the federal minimum wage?

Yes. The federal minimum wage has been $7.25/hour since 2009. Maine's minimum wage is higher, and employers covered by both laws must pay the higher of the two.

When does the new rate take effect?

January 1, 2027, alongside minimum wage increases in the other states tracked on this site.
