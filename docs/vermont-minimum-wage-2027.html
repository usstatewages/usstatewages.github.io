Frequently asked questions

Why isn't Vermont's exact 2027 rate available yet?

Annual inflation adjustment (set formula). States using this kind of formula typically calculate and publish the exact next-year figure between September and December of the prior year - Vermont hasn't published it yet, so this page shows the mechanism instead of a guessed number.

Does this apply to tipped workers?

Not necessarily. Many states, including Vermont, let employers pay tipped workers a lower direct cash wage as long as tips bring total pay up to at least the full minimum wage. The rate on this page is the standard rate for non-tipped workers - check your state labor department for the tipped cash wage.

Is this different from the federal minimum wage?

Yes. The federal minimum wage has been $7.25/hour since 2009. Vermont's minimum wage is higher, and employers covered by both laws must pay the higher of the two.

When does the new rate take effect?

January 1, 2027, alongside minimum wage increases in the other states tracked on this site.
