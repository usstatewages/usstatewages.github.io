How the 2027 rate is set

Switches from a legislated schedule to annual CPI-W (Northeast) indexing starting January 2027.

At the current $17.00/hr rate, a full-time worker (2,080 hours/year) earns about $35,360/year gross. This page will be updated with the 2027 rate and updated annual figure as soon as New York publishes it.
